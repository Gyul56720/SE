X38. Colour, the ISP Pipeline and Display Processing

Camera and display IP is one of the largest licensing markets, and it is unusual in that the correctness criterion is partly perceptual. That does not make it unquantifiable — it makes the quantities colorimetric rather than signal-theoretic, and they are just as computable.

Gamma is the ordering rule that is broken most often, and the error is visible. Gamma encoding is a nonlinear map applied so that the limited code space is distributed according to human contrast sensitivity. Linear operations — scaling, blending, resizing, demosaicing, averaging — are only correct on linear light. Resizing a gamma-encoded image darkens edges and shifts colours; alpha-blending gamma-encoded values produces halos. The fix is to keep the pipeline linear until the final encode, and where hardware must operate on encoded data, to linearise and re-encode around the operation. This is a real and recurring defect in shipped display and camera pipelines, and it is worth an explicit statement in an IP block's datasheet about which domain its input and output are in.

X38.2 Colour spaces as matrices

Why colour conversion is a 3×3 matrix and when it is not
StepJustification
1Human colour vision has three cone types, so colour is a three-dimensional quantity.Trichromacy. Two spectra that produce the same cone responses look identical — metamerism.
2A display's primaries span a triangle in chromaticity space; its gamut is that triangle.Any colour inside is reproducible; outside is not.
3Converting between two sets of primaries is a change of basis: a 3×3 matrix on linear tristimulus values.Linear is load-bearing — see X38.1.
4So BT.709 to BT.2020, or camera RGB to XYZ, is one matrix multiply per pixel.Nine multiplies and six adds — trivial hardware, which is why colour conversion is never the bottleneck.
5It stops being a matrix when the gamut does not contain the colour, or when the transfer function is not a power law.Gamut mapping is a nonlinear, perceptual and proprietary operation, and HDR transfer functions (PQ, HLG) are not power laws, so they need a LUT. Those two are where display IP actually differentiates.

X38.3 Line buffers: the memory that sets ISP area

Worked example 37. Why an ISP is mostly SRAM
Given. A 4K (3840-wide) pipeline, 12 bits per channel. The stages need vertical neighbourhoods: demosaic 5 lines, noise reduction 7, sharpening 5, lens shading 1, scaler 4.
Method. Each stage that looks at neighbouring rows must buffer those rows, because the sensor delivers one row at a time. Count the lines and the bits.

How this is got wrong. By counting arithmetic and not lines. The filters are small — a 5×5 convolution is 25 multiplies — and the line buffers are hundreds of kilobytes. Two consequences follow and both are architectural. Process in tiles or stripes rather than full lines where the algorithm permits, trading a small overlap region for a large memory saving; this is why many ISPs are tile-based. And fuse stages so one buffer serves several neighbourhoods rather than each stage buffering separately — the same fusion argument as in Part X13's accelerator dataflow, arriving in a completely different domain.

Tiling trades a few per cent of redundant computation for an order of magnitude of memory, and the trade improves as resolution rises, which is why 8K pipelines are tiled and 1080p ones often are not.
Problem. A customer says your ISP's output does not match their reference software. Where do you look?
Solution. Almost always at a convention rather than at the arithmetic, and the candidates are few enough to enumerate. Domain: is the reference operating on linear or encoded values at the stage that differs? X38.1's error is large and looks like a gain error. Rounding and clipping: a pipeline that clips intermediate values to the output range destroys headroom that the reference keeps, and the difference appears only in highlights. Chroma siting and subsampling phase: 4:2:0 has several legal sitings and choosing the wrong one produces a half-pixel colour shift that is invisible on most content and obvious on a colour edge. Matrix coefficients and range: limited versus full range, and BT.601 versus BT.709 coefficients, are four combinations and three of them are wrong. Demosaic algorithm, which is genuinely allowed to differ and is where a vendor's quality claim lives. Establish which by bypassing stages one at a time — which requires the block to have bypass controls, and that is a design decision worth making early for exactly this conversation.
