Semiconductor IP Design
Theory, Production Code, and the Practice of a Design House
A graduate curriculum for the design, modelling and verification of semiconductor IP blocks, written from first prin‐
ciples and grounded throughout in source code that is shipping in production silicon.
Corpus. 56,199 files, 19,925,868 lines of real design and modelling code were collected and indexed mechanically.
Every quantity quoted in this book is computed from that index; none is written from memory.
How to read. Green boxes recall undergraduate material; purple boxes carry graduate-level detail and the reasoning
a practising engineer needs; red boxes mark places where real projects have failed.
